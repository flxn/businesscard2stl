# Changelog
All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added
- Six layouts: Classic, Split, Accent bar, Centered, Minimal and Monogram.
- Style presets that set fonts and colors in one click.
- Name, job title, company and up to six contact details with icons (phone, email, website, social profiles, …).
- Logo: choose one of 48 icons or upload your own SVG.
- QR code with rounded or square modules, encoding your contact card (vCard), your website or any text, with a scannable preview and its printed module size.
- Six fonts, filament colors for card, text and accents, text sizes per element and a scale slider for all of them. Text that does not fit is reduced automatically, only in the block that is too big.
- Your last settings are saved in the browser and come back on your next visit. "Reset settings" restores the defaults (with undo).
- Card sizes for Europe, the US, Japan, credit cards, square or custom.
- Raised, engraved or flush inlaid details. Inlays can be printed face down for a smooth or textured front.
